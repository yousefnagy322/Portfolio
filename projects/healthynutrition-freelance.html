About the project

My contribution

  • Rebuilt the existing web experience as a cross-platform Flutter app for iOS and Android.
  • Published the Android app on Google Play; iOS release preparation is in progress.
  • Profiled rendering and refined widget rebuilds to improve responsiveness.
